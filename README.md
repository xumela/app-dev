# My Favorite Movie: Tangledd
![Tangled Movie Poster](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/a/a8/Tangled_poster.jpg)
## About the Movie
**Tangled** is one of my favorite Disney animated movies. It was released in 2010 and tells the story of a young princess named Rapunzel, who has magical long hair. She has spent most of her life locked inside a tower until she meets a thief named Flynn Rider, who helps her discover the world outside.
## Movie Details
- **Title:** Tangled
- **Release Year:** 2010
- **Genre:** Animation, Adventure, Comedy, Romance
- **Director:** Nathan Greno and Byron Howard
- **Production:** Walt Disney Animation Studios
- **Main Characters:** Rapunzel, Flynn Rider, Mother Gothel, and Pascal
## Why I Love This Movie
I love Tangled because it has a beautiful story, amazing songs, and lovable characters. Rapunzel inspires me because she is brave, curious, and never gives up on her dreams. I also love the relationship between Rapunzel and Flynn Rider because it shows the importance of trust, love, and taking risks.
## Favorite Characters
1. **Rapunzel** – A kind and adventurous princess who dreams of seeing the outside world.
2. **Flynn Rider** – A funny and charming thief who becomes Rapunzel's partner.
3. **Pascal** – Rapunzel's cute and loyal chameleon.
4. **Maximus** – A determined horse who adds humor to the story.
## My Favorite Quote
> "Venture outside your comfort zone. The rewards are worth it."
## Favorite Song
My favorite song from Tangled is **I See the Light** because it is romantic, beautiful, and meaningful.
## Conclusion
Overall, Tangled is my favorite movie because it teaches us to follow our dreams, be brave, and believe in ourselves. It reminds me that there is a beautiful world waiting for us if we are willing to take a chance and explore beyond our comfort zone.
